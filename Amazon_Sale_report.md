### Amazon Sale Data Analyst Dashboard | Power BI

#### Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze Amazon sales data, focusing on sales performance, geographical distribution, product categories, promotions, order status, and fulfilment. This report employs visualizations such as KPI cards, pie charts, stacked percentage bar charts, and scatter plots to help users quickly analyze Amazon sales performance.



#### Project Objectives

Monitor overall sales performance through key performance indicators.

Analyze sales distribution across different geographical areas and sales channels.

Explore order trends across product categories and styles.

Evaluate the impact of promotions on sales and average order value.

Analyze order distribution and customer purchasing patterns across different funnel stages.



#### Data Modeling

FactSales: Main sales transaction data, including order details, sales amount, quantity, and transaction information.

DimArea: Area information and area classification.

DimDate: Date attributes for time-based sales analysis.

DimProduct: Product information and product categories.

DimPromotion: Promotion details and discount-related information.

DimShipping :Shipping methods and delivery-related information.

#### Final Conclusion

Amazon's apparel business is substantial and stable, yet highly concentrated. It has generated approximately $5.1 billion in sales from roughly 120,000 orders, though order volume saw a decline between April and June. Market demand is heavily concentrated in the southern region and major metropolitan areas, while service coverage in many other regions remains insufficient. Revenue relies significantly on a small number of specific apparel sets, standard-sized products, and two particular promotional campaigns. B2B orders and plus-size apparel represent high-value market segments. Fulfillment performance is generally strong—with 55% of sales fulfilled by Amazon, primarily via expedited shipping—but the order cancellation rate stands at approximately 14%. Key areas for future improvement include reducing the order cancellation rate, restoring order growth, expanding into new regional markets, and driving growth within the B2B segment.
